## Biggest group, and the one change

**Unsupported or misleading claims: 3 of 11 distinct, 6 of 15 served** (one of them is the chapter shared 4 times). Two of the three sit in the opening card: the writer reaches for a strong hook and says more than the research holds (an absolute "the single biggest threat", a date the brief never gave, a cause the facts don't support). One had a fact check that softened the next card and left the hook; one had a fact check that let the date through; one is a pre-written chapter with no stored check.

The change: **the fact check lists every date, number, cause-and-effect claim and absolute word ("always", "never", "the biggest") that isn't in the research facts, opening card first, and rewrites or cuts each one.** Add the case to the true-facts rubric first ("L1 also covers a hook that claims more than the research"), then write one test case per example above.

The "Do it now" ordering bug is the cheapest fix of the lot: a quick handbook's first checklist is moved to the front whether or not it's a "What you need" list. Fix today, one test case.
