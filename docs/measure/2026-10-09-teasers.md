# Teaser repair, 9 Oct 2026 (night)

Shaktimaan's walk-through found Western philosophy's chapter teasers (the plan's "hook" lines, shown on the path, in the preview and on Up next) describing the chapter after them. A check of all 24 ready topics (25 stored plans: Homer has two) against each chapter's own text found 26 teasers in 9 topics that did not match their chapter. The other 15 topics match.

Cause: the plan writer wrote some hooks as "Next:" teasers for the following chapter (Western philosophy's chapter 7 even ends "Next: Wittgenstein", a chapter that does not exist), and a few hooks promised numbers or names the chapter never uses. Nothing compared a hook with its own chapter.

Repair: `repairData.setHooks` (internal) changes only `plan.chapters[i].hook`, in every stored copy of the topic and in readers' copies, and only where the hooks still read exactly as before. No chapter text changed, no model call. A teaser is either moved to the chapter that opens on it, or lifted from its own chapter's text. Dev first, then prod: 40 stored copies and 120 readers' copies changed; a second run changed nothing; every other field of the 110 stored rows is identical. To undo, run it with `from` and `to` swapped.

The full `from` and `to` lists, as run, are in 2026-10-09-teasers.json (`npx convex run --prod repairData:setHooks "$(cat docs/measure/2026-10-09-teasers.json)"`). Each change, with its reason:
