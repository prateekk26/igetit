## Leaks, biggest first

1. Nothing brings readers back: 3 of 43 returned; 0 reminders ever set; no email for visitors.
2. Chapter 2 opens with a quiz on chapter 1, minutes after chapter 1 (0 of 7 who skipped it finished).
3. Chapter 2 is longer than chapter 1 in a third of the Shelf books.
4. Many chapter 1 passes are samplers flicking to the end (a pass is reaching the last page).
5. Chapter 1 ends on homework, not its payoff.
6. The wall after chapter 2 converts about 29% of those who reach it; few reach it.
