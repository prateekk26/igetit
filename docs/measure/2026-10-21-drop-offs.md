## Where exactly they leave

1. **First screen, first seconds.** 33 of 57 (58%) left within 15 s (22 mobile, 11 desktop). Of the 43 who never started a book, 32 sent nothing after their first 6 s and 23 never got past the hero. Heartbeats are every 30 s, so "under 30 s" is as precise as it gets.
2. **The demo.** 17 saw the "Take a page" section; 2 tapped it; 0 started a book from it.
3. **The box.** 10 tapped it, 6 left without typing. The 4 who typed all reached the goal screen and the wait; chapter 1 opened 166 s and 352 s after their submit (two measured).
4. **Chapter 1.** 6 of 14 who opened it did not finish. 3 left on page 1, 1 on page 2, 2 on page 3 or 4. 3 of the 6 closed the chapter to the handbook page and left from there (within 10 s to 3 min). Chip taps open chapter 1 in 1 to 3 s; time from landing to tap was 2 to 52 s (median about 10 s).
5. **Chapter 2.** 6 of 8 passes opened chapter 2 within about a minute (Done's Next works). Then 3 stopped on its first card or before any card (the recall questions on chapter 1; same as 0 of 7 in the 20 Oct note), 1 stopped on page 6, 2 finished (one of them flicked through in 72 s).
6. **Account and pay.** 3 accounts, 1 wall tap, 0 payments in the week.

## Biggest leaks, ranked by people lost

1. First screen: 33 lost in under 15 s.
2. Looked around, never started: 10 more scrolled (17 saw the demo) and left without a tap.
3. Chapter 1: 6 of 14, half from the handbook page after closing the chapter.
4. Chapter 2's first card: 3 of 6.
5. Box tapped, nothing typed: 6 of 10.
