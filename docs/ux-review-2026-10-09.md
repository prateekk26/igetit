# UX review of the whole app, 9 Oct 2026

## For future agent
A list of problems, not fixes; what happened to each is in "Status after the fixes" below. Prateek asked for "the UI and UX of the app... just identify the problems", then for responsiveness, broken or misleading links, and the returning reader's experience. Read "Fix first" before anything else. Prove an item on the current build before you change code: other sessions shipped D36, D37 and D38 during this review, and more will follow.

Tags on every item:
- **(seen)**: watched it happen on the live site.
- **(seen, dev)**: watched it happen on the dev copy (localhost:5173 on joyous-weasel-315).
- **(code)**: found by reading the code, not triggered live.
- **Still there**: re-checked on the live build after the 16:02 deploy (assets index-ByeGQLMU.js).

## Fix first
1. **[P0] Your handbooks: "Make a free account (optional)" does nothing.** Tapping it throws an error (`Cannot access 'st' before initialization`; App.tsx declares `signIn` after the screen's early return). This is the only sign-up button on the screen every returning visitor lands on. (seen, still there)
2. **[P0] The Shelf's jump chips throw the reader out.** "Spotlight", "The Awards", "Money"... are `#shelf-...` links. The app's Back handler reads the jump as Back and leaves the Shelf: to the handbook page, or to the landing page for a new visitor. (seen, still there)
3. **[P0] One-sitting recipe: the first tick forces quizzes on unread steps.** In Maggi, ticking "Gather your tools" and "Just right" passes the whole chapter. That pulls in two "Remember this? · from chapter 1" quizzes (water quantity, handling the noodles) on steps the cook hasn't read, and they must be answered to move on. Cause (code): `logSet` passes a quiz-free chapter on the first logged set; App.tsx then puts the recall quizzes in front. (seen, dev)
4. **[P1] One "Not now" makes free chapter 2 look locked everywhere.** After declining sign-up once, every handbook's page says "Chapter 2 opens with a free account. When you're ready.", and the only main button is "Read chapter 1 again". The flag is per browser tab, not per handbook (App.tsx `wallDeclined`). Seen on Pool swimming. (seen, still there)
5. **[P1] "Make a free account" after chapter 1 says chapter 2 needs an account.** The sign-in screen it opens is headed "Chapter 2 is free with an account." / "To open chapter 2 of...". Chapter 2 needs no account (D26). (seen, dev)
