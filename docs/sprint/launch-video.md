# Launch video: script, three hooks, shot list (agent draft, 11 Oct night)

From the handbook's Product Launch Video page: about a minute; a hook in the first 3 to 7 seconds written as "Imagine ___, while ___ plays, and I say ___"; two of the three story parts; the product shown the way a user uses it; end on a promise or an offer; post it, and put two more hooks out as trial reels. Prateek shoots it (phone, ₹200 tripod, a park for light); the agent can cut a visuals-only draft from screen recordings if he wants one. Follows the reel formula in memory (hook 0 to 3 s, one step, a rehook, a save/share payoff, one CTA, captions 2 s or more).

## The script (template filled)

Product: a short handbook written for you, one 5-minute chapter a day, for the thing you keep meaning to learn.
Who it's for: the 26-year-old in India with hundreds of saved reels and a course stuck at a few percent.

HOOK (first 3 to 7 seconds)
Imagine: a phone's Saved folder scrolling forever, reel after reel ("Learn Excel in 7 days", "Public speaking hacks", "How to invest"), while a slow ticking clock plays, and I say: "I've saved [your real count] reels about getting better. I've watched [n]." (Count your own Saved folder; never a made-up number.)
The surprise: the scroll stops on one reel and the screen turns into a handbook page, chapter 1, open.

STORY (two of three)
Emotional: "Every January I start something. By February it's a saved post." (Prateek's own line, in his words.)
External: "So I built I Get It. You type the thing, pick what it's for, and in about a minute you get chapter 1, written for you: researched, fact-checked, with something to tap by the third card."

THE MIDDLE (the product doing the one thing)
On a phone in hand, outdoors: type "public speaking" → "Hold a room for 10 minutes" opens → card 1 (Ananya's introduction) → card 3, tap the opening a stranger would repeat → the page shows why it sticks → Done screen: "Chapter 1 of 28".
Rehook at about 25 s: a beat drop on the tap, and the caption "That's day 1. Five minutes."

THE END
Promise: "Five minutes a day. You'll get it."
Offer: "Eleven complete courses are free right now. Chapter 1 needs no sign-up. Link in bio."

Length: about 55 to 60 seconds.

## Two more hooks (for trial reels or separate X posts)

1. Imagine a coffee shop table, my laptop open on a course page that says "4% complete", while the Windows error sound plays, and I say: "This is the [nth] course I've bought and not finished." (his real count)
2. Imagine my phone on the table showing "Chapter 1 of 28", the camera shot from below so the phone looks huge, while a drum roll plays, and I say: "An AI wrote me a 28-day course on public speaking. Day 1 took four minutes."
