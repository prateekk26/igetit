# The free ten

## For future agent
Prateek picks one topic per round from three offered; record the pick under Picked, keep the rest in the Pool, stop at ten. Nothing here is built yet.

Ten ready handbooks anyone can read start to finish, free (Prateek, 10 Oct: "make it free, the complete course ... a flavor of what's on offer"). Each shows a vision title on the shelf, not the subject (Prateek: "something like what MasterClass does but in a fun bite-sized manner"). Titles are (agent) placeholders until Prateek rewrites them.

Picked one at a time, three choices a round; the two not picked go back in the pool.

Open: keep the free sign-up at chapter 3 on these and lift only the daily limit (agent's recommendation, not yet answered).

## Picked

1. **Hold a room for 10 minutes** (Public speaking; Prateek, 10 Oct: "Hold a room for 10 minutes. It will be a 28 day thing". The showcase, built first. Agent default, not yet confirmed: a new handbook beside the old one, which stays only for the 7 Oct carousel link). Proof, our own: 47 of the last 14 days' starts, 44 opened chapter 1, 16 passed; 39 taps, next best 6. Part of that is placement (landing demo, first on Featured, the 7 Oct carousel).
2. **Build a system that lands your next job** (Job search, new). Round 1, over "Make ChatGPT and Claude do your boring work" and "Why 9 in 10 F&O traders lose money". Proof: 72% of Indian professionals job-hunting in 2026, 84% feel unprepared (LinkedIn); Naukri 9 crore+ jobseekers.
3. **Ask for a raise and get it** (Salary negotiation, new). Round 2, over "Make ChatGPT and Claude do your boring work" and "File your own tax return in one evening". Proof: 81% of Indians plan to ask for a raise in the next 12 months, up from 67% (ACCA 2026).
4. **Speak English without translating in your head** (Spoken English, new). Round 3, over "Why 9 in 10 F&O traders lose money" and "Get a credit score banks say yes to". Proof: English Connection 17M YouTube subscribers; Duolingo: India its largest English-learning market.
5. **Tell a good IPO from hype** (How Indian IPOs work, already on the shelf). Round 4, over "Make ChatGPT and Claude do your boring work" and "Overcome your stage fear". Proof: ₹1.95 lakh crore in 365+ IPOs in 2025, a record; ours: 6 starts, 3 passed. Needs the caution line and SEBI care.
6. **Start the thing you keep putting off** (Procrastination, new). Round 5, over "Why 9 in 10 F&O traders lose money" and "Make Excel do your work". Proof: weak (no India number; Warikoo's bestseller on the theme; self-improvement is the most-saved Reel niche). Prateek's pick on instinct; watch its numbers.
7. **Spot what India's past multibaggers had in common** (Multibaggers, new). Round 6, over "Get a credit score banks say yes to" and "Get your first pull-up". Proof: 23.4 crore demat accounts (July 2026); NSDL record 59.3 lakh new in FY26. Riskiest under SEBI's finfluencer rules: no prices from the last 3 months, never a stock named as a hint to buy, caution line on the cover.
8. **Why 9 in 10 F&O traders lose money** (F&O losses, new). Round 7, over "Make ChatGPT and Claude do your boring work" and "Make one habit stick for good". Proof: SEBI study, 91% of individual traders lost money in FY25, ₹1.05 lakh crore, ~₹1.1 lakh each, 96 lakh traders. Cite SEBI; teach no strategy. Third market topic of the ten.
9. **Make ChatGPT and Claude do your boring work** (AI at work, new). Round 8, over "File your own tax return in one evening" and "Get your first pull-up". Proof: India 100M weekly ChatGPT users, OpenAI's #2 market (Feb 2026); 92% of Indian knowledge workers use AI at work.
10. **Make your credit card pay you back** (Credit cards: rewards, cashback and your score, new). Round 9 picked "Get a credit score banks say yes to" over "Make Excel do your work" and "Get your first pull-up"; Prateek then asked for optimising credit card spends and chose to merge the two (10 Oct). Proof: 18.3 crore Indians have checked their CIBIL score, first-time checkers +27% a year (Dec 2025); 12.3 crore cards outstanding and ₹2.08 lakh crore spent in July 2026, above ₹2 lakh crore three months running ([1](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/credit-card-spends-hit-rs-208-trillion-in-july-stay-above-rs-2-trillion-for-third-month)). Teaches how to judge a card's rewards and keep the score high; never names a card to get (offers change monthly). Caution line on the cover.
11. **Plan to buy your next car** (Car buying, new). Prateek's own addition, 10 Oct ("don't worry about the cost for now, we just need to make them the best we can"). Proof: record 47 lakh passenger vehicles sold in FY26; first-time buyers were 51% of Maruti's Q4 FY26 sales; car loans at a five-year low of 8.5 to 11.5% ([1](https://www.drivespark.com/four-wheelers/2026/small-car-sales-rise-in-june-2026-as-first-time-buyers-surge-087827.html), [2](https://vahanbazaar.in/news/india-auto-fy2026-record-sales-all-segments-wrap)). Teaches how to decide and negotiate; never names a model to buy. Caution line on the cover.

All picked 10 Oct (ten, then an eleventh). Left in the pool: File your own tax return (seasonal, revisit before May), Make one habit stick, Get your first pull-up, Make Excel do your work.
