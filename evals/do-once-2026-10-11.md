# Does a "do it once" rule in the writer prompt lift the outcome lever? (11 Oct night, agent, dev only)

Why: the baseline eval run and the read-and-count pass (docs/evals/read-2026-10-11.md) found chapter 1s that explain and show but never have the reader do the thing (rubric r1, "delivers its outcome" L3). The ten hand-built handbooks, whose plans put a Try it card or a "Your turn" in every chapter, score L4.

How: convex/evalDoOnce.ts. For a test handbook that already has a plan and a research brief, chapter 1 is written twice on Gemini 3.8 Flash with the user message generateChapter sends: once with the live writer prompt (CHAPTER_PROMPT_V6), once with a "DO IT ONCE" paragraph appended. Each chapter is scored by the rubric scorer on outcome and facts. Two runs per arm. Nothing stored but the call log; the live prompt untouched.

|---|---|---|
| Chess with your nephew | outcome 3, facts 4 · outcome 3, facts 4 | outcome 3, facts 2 · outcome 3, facts 2 |

The writer follows the plan's blocks: when the plan gives no "try" or "tryit" block, the rule in the writer's prompt doesn't add one.
